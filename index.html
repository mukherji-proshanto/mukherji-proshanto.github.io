Course Applets

Interactive tools for Proshanto Mukherji's math courses at The Cambridge School of Weston. Each one opens in the browser, with nothing to install and no sign-in.

Analytic Geometry

  • Roots Ring
    Unit 2 · The nth roots of a complex number, and how raising one root to the nth power stretches and turns you back to w.

Linear Algebra

  • Rows and Columns in Motion
    Ax = b, read two ways: each row works out one coordinate of b, and the columns are where î and ĵ land.
  • Shape Through a Matrix
    Unit 5 · Type a 2×2 matrix and a shape, and see every point move at once: stretches, turns, flips, shears and flattening.
